Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026.

1. Site operator and contact

The site is operated by its publisher, who publishes the project through the GitHub profile shaman2525. Please do not send children’s personal information when contacting the site.

2. Exercise data

Exercises run locally in the browser. They do not require a child’s name, account or public score. The current session score does not create a user profile.

3. Language preference

The site may store the selected PL/EN language locally in the browser. This preference does not contain a name or contact details.

4. Hosting

When hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process standard technical data needed to deliver the page, such as IP address, request time and browser information.

5. Google AdSense

The site contains a Google AdSense integration (publisher ID pub-2654167674032555). Once the site is approved and ads are enabled, Google and its partners may process technical data for ad delivery, security, frequency control and reporting.

The site is directed to children. The publisher should mark the site/ad requests for the appropriate age treatment. With child-directed treatment, personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled by Google’s applicable mechanisms.

6. Consent in the EEA

For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a Google-compliant consent management solution should be configured where required. The publisher can configure privacy messages in AdSense Privacy & messaging.

7. Children’s data

The site does not ask children for their name, address, school, phone number, photograph or other identifying information. If accounts, forms or stored progress are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.

8. Changes

This policy may change if site features, hosting, advertising services or legal requirements change.

This page describes the site’s technical privacy setup and is not legal advice.
